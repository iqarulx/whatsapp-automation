About WhatsApp automation

This app connects to WhatsApp the same way WhatsApp Web does, using an unofficial library rather than WhatsApp's official Business API. It is not endorsed by, affiliated with, or supported by WhatsApp or Meta. WhatsApp actively restricts automated or bulk messaging on personal accounts, and using this tool carries a real risk of your WhatsApp number being temporarily or permanently limited or banned by WhatsApp — independent of anything this app does. Use it at your own risk, in moderation, and only for messaging you'd be comfortable doing manually.
